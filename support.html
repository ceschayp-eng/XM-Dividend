How do I add a holding?

Open Holdings, tap the plus button, and enter the ticker, name, number of shares, cost per share, current price, annual dividend per share, and payment schedule. XM Dividend does not fetch market prices, so review and update these values yourself.

How do I record a dividend or other income?

Open Income, tap the plus button, and choose Add payment. Enter the amount, date, income type, and an optional linked holding. Tap a payment in the ledger to edit or delete it.

How do I record an expense?

Open Income, tap the plus button, and choose Add expense. You can also use the plus button beside Expense ledger. Enter an amount, category, date, and optional note or receipt. Tap an expense to edit or delete it.

How is income coverage calculated?

Forward monthly income is annual dividend income divided by 12. It is compared with your monthly spending baseline. Set a baseline under More → Planning assumptions, or leave it at zero to use the average monthly expense total across the current six-month window, including months with no recorded expenses as zero.

Are growth paths forecasts?

No. Growth paths are arithmetic illustrations based on your current entries and the growth rate and time horizon you select. They are not predictions, recommendations, or guarantees of future income.

Where are my records and photographs stored?

They are stored in the App's local container on your iPhone. XM Dividend has no account system, developer server, or sync service. Device backups depend on your own Apple device and iCloud settings.

How do I attach or save a photograph?

When adding or editing a holding, payment, or expense, choose Choose photo or Take photo. Where a full-size preview is available, tap the save icon to save a copy to your photo library. Camera and photo permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

How do I export my data?

Open More → Export and choose CSV or JSON. The App creates the file locally and opens the iOS share sheet. Choose carefully where you send financial records. CSV and JSON exports contain record data, not attached photographs.

Can I restore an exported file?

The current App does not provide an import or cloud-restore feature. Keep exports for reference and portability. Before erasing data or uninstalling the App, confirm that you have any photographs and records you need.

How do I remove sample data or erase everything?

Open More → Your data → Erase all data. This permanently deletes local holdings, payments, expenses, goals, settings, and attached photographs. It does not delete files or images you previously exported.

What if the App shows a storage warning?

A storage warning means the saved file could not be read. The original file is preserved and changes are paused to avoid overwriting it. Contact support before erasing your data if you need help understanding the situation.
