Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how XM Dividend handles information when you use the XM Dividend iPhone application (the “App”) and this website.

Summary

XM Dividend does not require an account and does not operate a user-data server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share your financial records for our own purposes. The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

1. Information You Enter in the App

You may enter holdings, ticker symbols, prices, dividend amounts, payments, expenses, goals, notes, tax-rate assumptions, and other financial records. You may also attach photographs of notices, statements, confirmations, or receipts.

This information is stored only in the App's local container on your device. It is not transmitted to us. We do not maintain user accounts, cloud databases, or a synchronization service.

2. Camera and Photo Library Access

The App requests camera or photo-related access only for features you choose to use:

  • Take photo: photograph a supporting document and attach it to your record.
  • Choose photo: select an existing image to attach. The selected image is processed locally.
  • Save to Photos: save a copy of an attached image only when you explicitly tap the save button.

Attached images are resized and stored locally. Denying permission does not prevent you from using the App's manual record-keeping features.

3. Exports and Sharing

CSV and JSON exports are created locally as temporary files. They are shared only when you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. Photographs are likewise saved or shared only at your request.

If you send records or images to another app, cloud service, or person, that recipient's privacy policy applies. XM Dividend does not receive a copy of these exports.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or tracking cookies. We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage events, precise location, contacts, or diagnostic profiles from the App.

5. Device Backups

Your device or computer may include locally stored App data in backups, depending on your Apple device, Finder, and iCloud settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by XM Dividend. We do not have access to them. Review your backup settings if you do not want local App data included in a backup.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them, erase all data in the App, or uninstall the App. You can delete individual holdings, payments, expenses, goals, and attachments. Deleting a holding also deletes its linked payment records and their locally stored attachments.

Uninstalling the App removes its local container. Copies included in a device backup may remain until you delete the backup or it is overwritten. Files or images you have exported are not deleted by the App and must be managed in their destination.

7. Security

We rely on the local app-container and device-security protections provided by iOS. Use a device passcode and keep your operating system up to date. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so protect exported files and device backups appropriately.

8. This Website and External Links

This website provides product, privacy, support, and legal information. It does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, account registration, or tracking scripts.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the hosting service. GitHub's handling of information is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

External links, including email links and links opened in your browser, are subject to the policies of the services you choose to use.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because App records are stored locally and are not transmitted to us, parents and guardians should manage a child's device and App access directly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the App, website, or legal requirements. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is posted. Material changes may also be communicated through an App update or release notes where appropriate.
